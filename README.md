# My Favorite Series: *Stranger Things*

> "Friends don't lie." — Eleven

**Stranger Things** is an American sci-fi and horror show on Netflix created by the *Duffer Brothers*. The story takes place in the 1980s in a small town called **Hawkins, Indiana**. It follows a group of best friends who discover secret government labs and a dark, scary world called the **Upside Down**.

---

## Why I Love This Series
- **1980s Vibe:** Great music, cool retro clothes, and fun references to old movies and games.
- **Great Friendships:** The characters care deeply about each other, especially Eleven, Dustin, Steve, and Hopper.
- **Exciting Story:** Every season mixes scary monsters with funny and touching moments.

## Main Characters
1. **Eleven (Jane Hopper)** – A young girl who can move things with her mind.
2. **Mike Wheeler** – A loyal friend who always tries to protect the group.
3. **Dustin Henderson** – The smart and funny friend who loves science.
4. **Jim Hopper** – The town's police chief who takes care of Eleven like a daughter.
